Comet & Commander

Your Commander carrier sits at the centre of a sphere of space. Mine the comets and asteroids around you, build a fleet and destroy the three enemy hives before their waves destroy you.

Economy

  • Harvesters mine comets and asteroids on their own and carry the ore back to the Commander.
  • Spend ore on ships and upgrades. They are built one at a time in the Commander's queue.
  • The Commander repairs ships near it and defends itself with its turrets.

Mouse

  • Click a ship to select it, drag a box to select several, Shift to add, double-click for every ship of that type.
  • Right-click to order: space to move, an enemy to attack, a comet to mine. With the Commander selected, right-click sets a rally point.
  • Right-drag turns the view all the way round you, the wheel zooms. Ctrl+1-9 makes a group, 1-9 picks it.

Touch

  • Tap a ship to select it, then tap where it should go. Drag to look around, pinch to zoom.

Keys

  • F, C, H build. X army, Z harvesters, B base. WASD or arrows turn the view, Q/E zoom. P pause, M sound.
