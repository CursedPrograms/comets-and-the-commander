    Comets and the Commander

    How to play

    • Space is split into ten sectors, each held by a different enemy faction. Destroy every hive in a sector to conquer it, then conquer all ten.
    • Three resources, each with its own harvester: meteorites give steel, comets give water, the rarer green meteorites give fuel. Ships cost steel and fuel, buildings steel and water; fuel also powers black holes and the Pathfinder outside your spheres. Harvesters unload at the Commander or a refinery.
    • Spend ore on ships and on upgrades: weapons, hulls, mining and logistics (cheaper, faster ships). Everything is built in the Commander's queue.
    • The Commander repairs ships near it. Idle warships escort it when it moves.
    • Your ships only fight inside your sphere round the Commander. To attack a hive, fly the Commander to it.
    • Waves come often and grow; every fifth is a big one, and raiders come in from the edge of your sphere.
    • The other sectors raid you too, from the side they lie on: watch the edge of your sphere.
    • Rare comets streak in from any direction. Watch for the blinking line and get out of the way.
    • The Pathfinder is a scout you fly yourself (T, third person): W thrust, S brake, A/D turn, arrows pitch, Shift boost. It can leave your sphere (burning fuel) and plants black holes (Space): for 20 seconds they pull in and swallow enemy ships.
    • Three queues build at once: ships, base buildings, and research. Click an item in a queue (bottom panel) to cancel it, or right-click its button: everything it cost comes back.
    • Buildings float where they're built, round the Commander: solar plants make power, and sparks carry it to the rest. Short of power, everything works slower. Buy the Tractor and they travel with you.
    • A Tech Centre unlocks research, the Dreadnought (your very special ship) and the Usurper.
    • Two kinds of hive: spires you destroy, nests you capture. A nest can't be destroyed: send a Usurper (right-click the nest). A captured nest is a sphere of your empire and builds its faction's ships for you. Spheres (J) jumps between them.
    • Every faction has a flagship with its own trick, and some sectors have an Overlord: an enemy commander with miners, refineries and solar plants, who builds a fleet and attacks like you do. Each faction can field 250 ships; so can you (plus 20 harvesters).

    Mouse

    • Click a ship to select it, drag a box for several, Shift adds, double-click picks every ship of that type.
    • Right-click to order: space to move, an enemy to attack, a comet to mine. Select the Commander and right-click to fly the carrier.
    • Right-drag turns the view all the way round, the wheel zooms. Ctrl+1-9 makes a group, 1-9 picks it.

    Touch

    • Tap a ship, then tap where it should go. Drag to look around, pinch to zoom.

    Keys

    • F, C, H build, G Dreadnought, U Usurper, Y Pathfinder. T flies the Pathfinder, Space plants a black hole while flying. J jumps between your spheres. X army, Z harvesters, B Commander. N sector map. WASD or arrows turn the view, Q/E zoom. P pause, M sound, Esc menu.
