    Comets and the Commander

    How to play

    • Space is split into ten sectors, each held by a different enemy faction. Destroy every hive in a sector to conquer it, then conquer all ten.
    • Harvesters mine comets and asteroids near the Commander and bring the ore home. Build more of them early.
    • Spend ore on ships and on upgrades: weapons, hulls, mining and logistics (cheaper, faster ships). Everything is built in the Commander's queue.
    • The Commander repairs ships near it. Idle warships escort it when it moves.
    • Your ships only fight inside your sphere round the Commander. To attack a hive, fly the Commander to it.
    • Waves come often and grow; every fifth is a big one, and raiders come in from the edge of your sphere.
    • The other sectors raid you too, from the side they lie on: watch the edge of your sphere.
    • Rare comets streak in from any direction. Watch for the blinking line and get out of the way.

    Mouse

    • Click a ship to select it, drag a box for several, Shift adds, double-click picks every ship of that type.
    • Right-click to order: space to move, an enemy to attack, a comet to mine. Select the Commander and right-click to fly the carrier.
    • Right-drag turns the view all the way round, the wheel zooms. Ctrl+1-9 makes a group, 1-9 picks it.

    Touch

    • Tap a ship, then tap where it should go. Drag to look around, pinch to zoom.

    Keys

    • F, C, H build. X army, Z harvesters, B Commander. N sector map. WASD or arrows turn the view, Q/E zoom. P pause, M sound, Esc menu.
